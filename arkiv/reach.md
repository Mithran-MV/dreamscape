# Reach

## Who it's for

People who remember their dreams for ten seconds after waking and then lose them. Dreamscape asks for one tap and a few spoken words, then gives back a painting, a comic in your own words, and a gallery you can let your family visit for a while.

Hackers at ETHGlobal Mumbai are the first group: the `ethglobal-mumbai` circle paints a shared World Dream from the themes people at the venue told that night.

## Can you try it today

- Clone the repo and run it on your laptop with the steps in the README.
- Read the market straight from Arkiv with the query in `schema.md`; it needs no account.
- On the phone, the app installs from the browser (it's a web app with a manifest and a service worker).
